PURPOSE

Technology that protects the source.

The Quran is never treated as generic application content. Canonical Arabic text, translations, tafsir, recitation metadata, and provider-supplied content remain clearly attributed and separate from personal notes or generated assistance.

01

Canonical content remains intact

Source text is displayed without automated rewriting. Provider edition, version, and attribution stay visible and auditable.

02

Controlled synchronization

Provider updates are designed to pass through validation and administrator review before becoming active in Jarvis.

03

Privacy before personalization

Optional bookmarks, notes, and reading progress remain disabled until clear consent and appropriate safeguards are available.

CONTENT & INDEPENDENCE

Built with acknowledged sources.

Jarvis Quran may retrieve Quranic content through Quran Foundation APIs under the provider's developer terms. Jarvis Quran is an independent application and is not an official Quran Foundation or Quran.com product.

Quran data provided by Quran.Foundation. Specific translations, tafsir editions, and recitations are credited by resource when displayed.
