Jarvis Quran (the “Service”) is a private, non-commercial, invitation-only feature within Jarvis, operated by Abdul Hamied (“we”, “us”, or “the Operator”) for authorized Jarvis Team members. This policy explains how information is handled when the Service is used.

Independent service. Jarvis Quran is not an official Quran Foundation, Quran.com, or QuranReflect application. It uses Quran Foundation APIs in accordance with the provider's developer terms.

1. Scope and current integration

The Service currently uses application-level API access for public Quranic content. Quran Foundation user login is not enabled. Accordingly, Jarvis Quran does not access a user's Quran.com bookmarks, notes, collections, goals, reading sessions, or Quran Foundation account profile.

Public Quran text and associated metadata may be retrieved through Quran Foundation APIs without collecting personal user information from Quran Foundation.

2. Information we process

CategoryExamples and purpose
Jarvis account informationName, username, work email, profile image, and access role used to authenticate authorized users and enforce access controls.
Security and technical recordsLogin time, IP address, browser or device information, request status, and audit events used for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.
Optional Quran activityBookmarks, personal notes, reading position, history, or progress only when the relevant feature is enabled and the user gives explicit consent.
Public Quranic contentQuran text, translations, tafsir references, recitation metadata, and structural metadata retrieved from approved sources.

Sensitive religious information

Personal notes, reflections, bookmarks, and reading patterns can reveal religious beliefs and are treated as sensitive information. Such features will remain disabled until the Service presents a separate, unticked opt-in consent and the required safeguards are active. Refusing optional consent will not remove access to the core reading experience.

3. How information is used

  • Provide authenticated Quran reading and source-aware navigation.
  • Protect access to a private, invitation-only service.
  • Save optional personal reading features after explicit consent.
  • Maintain security, integrity, availability, and an auditable update process.
  • Investigate errors, abuse, or security incidents.

Prohibited uses

We do not sell or rent personal information, build advertising profiles, mine religious activity for unrelated purposes, or use personal notes and reflections to train artificial-intelligence models without separate, specific consent. Quran Foundation user data is not repurposed beyond disclosed features.

4. Service providers and data sharing

We disclose only the minimum information needed to operate the Service. We do not sell personal information.

  • Quran Foundation — provides Quranic content through backend API calls. Jarvis Quran does not send personal notes, bookmarks, or reading history to Quran Foundation while user login remains disabled. Provider privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts only these public informational and legal pages. It does not host the Jarvis Quran application database or private reading data. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Operator-managed Jarvis infrastructure — processes authenticated application requests and, if enabled with consent, stores optional reading information.

Service providers are used under their applicable agreements and are not authorized by us to sell Jarvis Quran user data or misuse Quranic content.

5. Security and incident response

Jarvis Quran is designed around backend-only API credentials, least-privilege access, authenticated roles, audit records, encryption in transit, protected credential storage, controlled secret rotation, and restricted administrative access. Optional sensitive reading-data features will not be enabled until the corresponding security controls and consent flow have been verified.

Actual or suspected unauthorized access, data exposure, or API-related security incidents will be investigated promptly. Incidents related to Quran Foundation APIs will be reported to the provider within 24 hours where required. Our practices are intended to align with the Quran Foundation Developer Privacy Policy Packet, including its Security Rule 6.9 reference.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Users should avoid placing unrelated confidential information in Quran notes.

6. Your rights and controls

Authorized users may request to access, correct, export, or delete their personal Jarvis Quran data. Requests can be made by email to duduabdulhamied@gmail.com. Identity may be verified before fulfilling a request.

Because Quran Foundation user login is currently disabled, there is no Quran Foundation OAuth token for a Jarvis Team member to revoke. If user login is enabled in the future, this policy and the Service will be updated with token-revocation and in-app deletion controls before activation.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Account and optional reading data are retained while access is active and the relevant feature is required.
  • Following a verified deletion request, personal data will be removed from live systems within 30 days.
  • Residual copies in protected backups will expire or be removed within 90 days.
  • Security records may be retained for a limited period where necessary to investigate incidents or meet legal obligations.
  • Provider-managed Quran content is retained and synchronized only as permitted by the relevant source terms.

8. Children

Jarvis Quran is intended for adult, authorized Jarvis Team members and is not directed to children under 18. It does not provide public registration. If underage access is identified, access and associated personal data will be removed as appropriate.

9. International data transfer

Quran Foundation and GitHub may process limited network or service data outside Indonesia. Public legal pages are globally accessible. Private reading data is not intentionally published through these services. We use provider agreements and applicable legal safeguards for cross-border processing and seek to minimize transferred data.

10. Policy changes and contact

Material updates will be communicated through Jarvis, email, or another appropriate notice before they take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

Privacy contact duduabdulhamied@gmail.com Abdul Hamied · Bengkulu, Indonesia We aim to respond to privacy enquiries within 30 days.
